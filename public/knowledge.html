MyZubster comic

A visual language for continuous improvement

The comic expresses the project’s narrative: observe a problem, analyse it, correct it, test it and repeat. It does not report a live deployment, audit, PR or operational metric.

Illustrative visual · not evidence

Read it as a principle, not a dashboard. Labels inside this comic—including audit, CI, deployment and PR references—are narrative examples. The implementation status of a real system must come from inspectable repositories, measurements and independent review.

This distinction is part of the interface: attractive visuals are useful for orientation, but they never replace evidence.

What we learned

Evidence before claims

Integrity is not scientific validity.Hashes and timestamps can protect a record from unnoticed change; they do not prove a physical or clinical outcome.
Experience is valuable input.User observations can reveal patterns for research and design, without becoming medical conclusions or product-performance proof.
Pilots need gates.Collection, material treatment and recovered-output claims need documented methods, measurements, permissions and external review where appropriate.
How to participate

Contribute responsibly

1. Share an observationDescribe context, limits, consent and uncertainty. Do not publish personal or health-sensitive information.
2. Convert a pattern into a protocolDefine a question, method, measurement and reviewer boundary.
3. Test and reviewOnly measured and independently reviewed results may support material public claims.
