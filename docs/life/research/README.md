# MyZubster Living Lab — Research Layer

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR RESEARCH ENROLMENT

This directory defines a prospective research layer for MyZubster Living Lab activities.

It is intentionally separate from:

- Marketplace participation;
- contributor status;
- MyZubster/Zorgax pilot participation;
- stakeholder or partner status;
- financial/payment activity;
- blockchain evidence.

## Core rule

Marketplace use, contribution activity, collaboration or pilot consent MUST NOT be interpreted as research consent.

Research participation requires a separate participant-specific informed-consent process.

## Evidence separation

Operational events may exist independently of research:

    LISTING
      -> ACCEPTED
      -> HANDED_OVER
      -> RECEIVED
      -> RECORDED

Only events belonging to an appropriately enrolled research participant may later become eligible research evidence under an approved protocol.

## Blockchain boundary

Research consent and participant identity MUST NOT be written on-chain.

A technical event may have a cryptographic commitment where appropriate, but that commitment is not proof of research consent.

## Current status

No person is enrolled in research by this document.

Existing participant, contributor and candidate states remain unchanged.

The research protocol, participant information sheet and research consent materials require human and, where applicable, institutional/ethics review before research enrolment begins.
