## Activation gate

A candidate becomes a research participant only after:

1. research scope has been defined;
2. participant has received adequate research information;
3. participation is explicitly voluntary;
4. research-specific consent has been obtained;
5. a pseudonymous research identifier has been assigned;
6. data minimisation requirements have been checked;
7. human approval has been recorded.

Until all gates are satisfied:

RESEARCH PARTICIPANT = NO
